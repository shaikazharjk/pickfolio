Best Mechanical Gaming Keyboards Under $100 (Budget, Mid, and Beyond)

A good mechanical keyboard feels and sounds obviously better — and you don't need $200+ to get one. Three picks, all under $100.

What actually matters

Switch type. Linear is smooth and popular for gaming; tactile has a bump, better for typing.

Hot-swappable sockets. Lets you try different switches later without soldering.

Build style. Gasket mount feels softer; tray mount feels stiffer and louder.

Keycap material. PBT resists the shine ABS develops after a few months.

Quick FAQ

Linear or tactile for gaming? Personal preference — linear for fast inputs, tactile to feel each keystroke.

Do I need hot-swap sockets? Only if you'll want to try different switches later.

Are budget boards loud? Depends more on switch type and case than price.

Wired or wireless for gaming? Wired avoids any theoretical latency; wireless is fine for most people.
