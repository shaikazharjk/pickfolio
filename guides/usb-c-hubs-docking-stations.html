Best USB-C Hubs and Docking Stations for a Desk Setup

Hub or dock? A confusing category, sorted out — three picks by tier.

Hub vs. dock — the real difference

A hub is smaller and covers the basics. A docking station has more ports, more power, and often multiple displays. One monitor and a couple peripherals? Get a hub. Multi-monitor setup that needs to charge your laptop? Get a dock.

What actually matters

Power delivery (PD) wattage. Check it against your laptop's charging wattage.

Display support. Hubs usually drive one display; docks often drive two or three at 4K60.

DisplayLink vs. native. DisplayLink drives extra displays well for work, less ideal for gaming.

Quick FAQ

Will any hub work with any laptop? Check your port supports video output (DisplayPort Alt Mode or Thunderbolt).

Do I need Thunderbolt for a dock? Not necessarily — Thunderbolt just supports more bandwidth.

Can a hub damage my laptop? A reputable one, used within spec, is safe.

Do Mac and Windows need different hubs? Mostly no — most are cross-platform.
