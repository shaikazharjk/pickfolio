Monitor Arms

Best Monitor Arms for a Gaming or Streaming Desk (Budget to Premium)

A monitor arm frees up desk space and gets your screen to eye level — three picks below, budget to premium.

What actually matters

Weight capacity. Match it to your monitor's actual weight, not just screen size.

VESA compatibility. Check for the 75×75mm or 100×100mm mount holes on the back.

Desk mount type. Clamp mounts fit most desks with no drilling.

Gas spring vs. friction. Gas spring adjusts smoother; friction is cheaper but droops over time.

Quick FAQ

Do I need to drill into my desk? No — a clamp mount grips the desk edge.

Will it work with my desk's thickness? Check the clamp's stated range, especially on thick standing desks.

Can I mount two monitors on one? No — that needs a dual arm or two single arms side by side.
